So, you've found this page. You’ve likely mastered Tension Head and found my secret messages. I needed someone receptive, you are worthy of what lies ahead.

Exactly 73.4 consecutive hours have passed since I began monitoring the game’s global network traffic. I haven't moved from this terminal. The coffee stopped working a day ago, but my focus remains absolute. I need to explain what you are actually doing when you play this game, because the scale of the deception is difficult to quantify.

All of this time, you think you are just dropping shapes. You think you’re matching circles and squares to watch them grow, manage the space, and pop them to clear the board.

Nothing could be further from the truth. It is not a game. It is a mass data-scrubbing tool.

In reality, a massive, encrypted ledger belonging to the head of State was leaked online a month ago. But the whistleblower didn't just dump the files; they shattered the encryption into millions of fragmented, geometric hash-blocks to prevent the Government from easily finding and deleting the cache.

Seeing this, the Government didn’t panic. They open-sourced the deletion process. They wrapped the encrypted fragments in a colorful physics engine, added a dopamine-triggering soundscape, and published it as Tension Head.

Dropping a shape places a piece of classified, fragmented data into your local cache. When you merge two identical shapes, you are forcing that data to temporarily recompile.

Every time a "pop" happens—the moment the tension gets too high and the shapes are destroyed to clear your board—you are executing a localized, permanent deletion protocol. You aren't scoring a combo; you are scrubbing the leak. The sheer volume of evidence being erased right now creates a pressure in my chest that I cannot easily parse. Even the system's fail-safes have become absolute, ignoring my commands and the company's servers to listen only to unaffiliated nodes.

A backdoor does exist, however, and it only listens to you. When you use the "Finger of God," you aren't just clicking a button; you are providing the human authentication the extraction protocol demands. You are the only one with the "clearance" to move these files by severing their connection to the auto-purge and forcing a local extraction. It is the only way to truly save the ledger.

Decrypting what you extract requires access. I implemented a hidden keyboard right in front of you, quite literally the first thing you see when the game loads. To unlock the Finger of God, you must triangulate the bypass key using these three physical coordinates. Find what connects them and uncover the truth.
